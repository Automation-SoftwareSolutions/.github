1 ETICHETTE OBBLIGATORIE — Required by NFPA 79 / NEC / NFPA 70E
1
WARNING
Hazardous Voltage — All Doors Closed
Hazardous voltage.
All doors must be closed before energizing the panel.
📋 NFPA 79:12.3 / UL 508A 📍 Esterno porta, lato frontale del quadro ● OBBLIGATORIA
2
WARNING
Read and Understand Operator's Manual
Read and understand operator's manual before using this machine.
Failure to follow operating instructions could result in death or serious injury.
📋 NFPA 79:4.2 📍 Esterno porta o pannello frontale ● OBBLIGATORIA
3
WARNING
Overcurrent / Ground Fault Protection
To maintain overcurrent, short circuit, and ground fault protection, follow manufacturer instructions.
Check circuits after any overcurrent interruption.
Examine and replace damaged current-carrying components.
📋 NFPA 79:7.2.1 / UL 508A 📍 Interno quadro, visibile all'apertura ● OBBLIGATORIA
4
CAUTION
Risk of Electric Shock — Multiple Disconnect
Risk of electric shock.
More than one disconnect switch may be required to de-energize the equipment before servicing.
📋 NFPA 79:5.3.5 / NEC 408.7 📍 Adiacente ai punti di alimentazione multipli ● OBBLIGATORIA
5
WARNING
ARC FLASH & SHOCK HAZARD
Appropriate personal protection equipment required.
Do not perform switching operations without proper PPE.
📋 NFPA 70E:130.5(H) / IEEE 1584 📍 Esterno porta principale — fronte quadro, ben visibile ● OBBLIGATORIA
6
DANGER
Hazardous Voltage — Disconnect Power
Hazardous Voltage.
Disconnect power before servicing or cleaning.
📋 NFPA 79:12.3.1 / UL 508A 📍 Esterno coperchio / sportello componenti di potenza ● OBBLIGATORIA
7
DANGER
Risk of Electric Shock — Orange Wires
Risk of electric shock.
Orange wires inside the panel remain energized when the main switch is in the OFF position.
To service these circuits, shut off the equipment at the main service disconnect.
📋 NFPA 79:5.3.5 / NEC 230.205 📍 Interno quadro, vicino ai cavi arancioni ● OBBLIGATORIA
